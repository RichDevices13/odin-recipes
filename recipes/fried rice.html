Nigerian Fried Rice

Description

Nigerian Fried Ricea vibrant, flavorful rice dish cooked with rich liver or chicken stock, mixed vegetables, liver, and aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups Parboiled Rice
  • Chicken stock and diced liver
  • Mixed vegetables (carrots, sweet corn, green peas, green beans)
  • Onions, thyme, and curry powder
  • Vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Parboil rice with curry, thyme, and salt, then drain.
  2. Cook rice in rich chicken stock until tender and liquid absorbs.
  3. Sauté chopped liver, onions, and mixed vegetables in oil with seasonings.
  4. Add cooked rice in small batches, stir-frying continuously on medium heat until well combined. Serve hot!
