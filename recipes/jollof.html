Jollof Rice

Description

Nigerian Jollof Riceis a dish with Jollof rice, fried plantain, roasted chicken, salad and some vegetables.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 scotch bonnet
  • 1 onion (blended)
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tsp curry powder and 1 tsp thyme
  • 2 seasoning cubes and salt to taste
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • Roasted chicken legs
  • 2 ripe plantains (sliced and fried)
  • Coleslaw (cabbage, carrots, corn, mayonnaise)

Steps In Cooking

  1. Blend tomatoes, red bell peppers, scotch bonnet peppers, and onions until smooth.
  2. Fry chopped onions and tomato paste in hot oil, then add the blended pepper mixture and cook until thickened.
  3. Season the sauce with curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, seasoning cubes, salt, and chicken stock.
  4. Add washed parboiled rice, cover tightly with foil and a lid, and steam on low heat for 25–30 minutes until tender.
  5. Roast seasoned chicken legs, fry sliced ripe plantains until golden brown, and mix cabbage, carrots, corn, and mayo for the coleslaw.
  6. Serve the rice with a fork and serve hot alongside the roasted chicken, plantains, and coleslaw.
