Ghanaian Jollof Rice

Description

Ghanaian Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish cooked in a flavorful, spiced tomato sauce. It is famous for using aromatic perfumed rice like Jasmine rice, giving it a distinct taste and texture.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Jasmine rice
  • Blend of tomatoes, red bell peppers, and scotch bonnet peppers
  • Tomato paste
  • Onions and garlic
  • Chicken broth or stock cubes
  • Vegetable oil

Steps

  1. Blend tomatoes, peppers, onions, and garlic until smooth.
  2. Heat oil in a pot and sauté finely chopped onions until soft.
  3. Add the tomato paste and stir-fry for a few minutes to remove raw acidity.
  4. Pour in the blended pepper mixture and let it stew until reduced and thickened.
  5. Add chicken stock and seasoning, then stir in washed Jasmine rice.
  6. Cover tightly with foil and a lid, and let it steam on low heat until the rice is tender. Serve with grilled chicken or fried plantains!
